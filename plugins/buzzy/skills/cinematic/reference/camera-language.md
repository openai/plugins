# Cinematic — Camera Language

写词时从本表取**具体运镜 + 速度**，不要写 "camera moves"。一层镜头叠 2–3 个动作即可。

## Foundational Techniques

**1. Establishing Shot (4–6s)**
"Establishing shot: camera positioned 50 feet from subject. Wide focal length equivalent (24–35mm cinema). Pan slowly across landscape revealing scale, depth, three distinct layers: foreground detail, mid-ground interest, background context."

**2. Push-In / Dolly Forward (1–3s)**
"Camera dolly forward 3 feet at constant speed over 2 seconds. Maintain shallow depth of field. Subject remains centered. Slight lens distortion at frame edges suggesting movement."

**3. Pull-Back / Reveal (1–4s)**
"Camera pulls back 20 feet over 3 seconds. Maintain sharp focus throughout. Foreground subject stays anchored, background reveals environmental scale."

**4. Whip Pan (0.3–0.6s)**
"Whip pan left to right across scene in 0.5 seconds. Motion-blur trails permitted. Subject B comes into sharp focus as camera settles. Smooth deceleration at end."

**5. Parallax / Depth Layer Move (2–4s)**
"Camera tilts upward at 2 feet per second. Foreground remains sharp, background moves slower, mid-ground at medium speed."

**6. Handheld / Steadicam (2–6s)**
"Handheld camera following subject. Micro-vibrations: 0.5–1mm frame jitter. Breathing motion (expand/contract frame 2–3 pixels). Steadicam-like smoothness with human imperfection. NOT locked-off rigid."

**7. Tracking Shot (3–8s)**
"Camera tracks subject from side profile. Maintain consistent framing: subject at frame-right, environment at frame-left. Move in sync with subject motion at 3 mph equivalent."

**8. Crane Up (3–6s)**
"Camera rises vertically 40 feet over 4 seconds. Subject remains in frame bottom-center initially. Reveals landscape as crane rises. Slight tilt down to maintain subject connection."

**9. 360 / Orbital Spin (4–8s)**
"Camera orbits 270 degrees around subject over 5 seconds. Maintain constant 10-foot distance. Subject always in frame-center. Counterclockwise rotation."

**10. Rack Focus (0.5–2s)**
"Rack focus from foreground (sharp, 2 feet away) to background (sharp, 30 feet away) over 1.5 seconds. Middle ground blurs during transition. f/1.4 cinema equivalent."

**11. Dutch Angle (3–8s)**
"Frame tilted 25 degrees counterclockwise. Horizon line diagonal across frame. Maintain this tilt throughout the clip. Conveys psychological imbalance."

**12. Insert / Extreme Close-Up (1–3s)**
"Extreme close-up of [object detail]. Fills 80% of frame. Shallow depth of field creates bokeh background."

**13. Over-the-Shoulder (2–4s)**
"Over-the-shoulder framing: camera positioned 2 feet behind character. Partial shoulder/head of foreground character visible frame-left, subject visible frame-right. Soft focus on near shoulder."

**14. Bird's Eye / Top-Down (2–5s)**
"Top-down camera position: directly above subject, 15 feet up. Looking straight down at 0-degree angle."

**15. POV (2–6s)**
"First-person camera position at eye level. Camera moves as if character walking. Frame jitters slightly with footsteps. Maintains head-height positioning throughout."

## Movement Encyclopedia

| Movement | Purpose | Typical | Prompt phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dolly Forward | Intimacy, tension | 1–3s | "Camera dolly forward at constant 2 feet/second. Maintain subject center-frame. Slight lens breathing. No focus shift." |
| Dolly Backward | Reveal context | 1–4s | "Camera pulls back 15 feet at 3 feet/second. Maintain subject in frame-center. Background gradually reveals." |
| Truck Left / Right | Lateral environment | 2–4s | "Camera trucks left 10 feet at 2 feet/second. Subject remains frame-right. Parallax: background slower than foreground." |
| Pan Left / Right | Rotate in place | 0.5–2s | "Camera pan left at 30 degrees per second. Smooth acceleration and deceleration. Sweeps 60° total." |
| Tilt Up / Down | Vertical reveal | 1–3s | "Camera tilts upward from feet to face at 20 degrees per second. Faster at start (30°/s), decelerates at end (10°/s)." |
| Whip Pan | Energetic transition | 0.3–0.6s | "Whip pan from subject A to subject B in 0.5 seconds. 90 degrees per second. Motion blur acceptable." |
| Handheld | Documentary urgency | 2–6s | "Handheld following subject. 0.5–1 mm frame jitter at 2 Hz. Breathing 1–2 pixels/s. NOT locked-off." |
| Steadicam / Gimbal | Liquid-smooth follow | 3–8s | "Gimbal-smooth follow. 3-foot distance from walking subject. Stabilization removes micro-vibrations." |
| Tracking / Side Follow | Subject in space | 3–6s | "Tracks from 4-foot side distance. Subject frame-right. Sync to 2 mph walk." |
| Crane Up | Scale / awe | 3–6s | "Rises vertically 30 feet over 4 seconds. Subject visible in lower frame. Tilt down slightly." |
| Crane Down | God-view to human | 2–4s | "Descends 20 feet over 3 seconds. Start wide-overhead, end at eye level." |
| 360 Orbit | All angles | 4–8s | "Orbits 270 degrees counterclockwise over 5 seconds. Constant 8-foot distance. 54°/s." |
| Spiral | Dreamlike | 4–8s | "Spirals upward and around. Rise 15 feet, orbit 180 degrees, both over 5 seconds." |
| Rack Focus | Guide attention | 0.5–2s | "Rack from sharp foreground (2 ft) to sharp background (25 ft) over 1.5s." |
| Zoom | Use sparingly | 1–3s | "Zoom 35mm to 85mm over 2 seconds. Feels false, use sparingly." |
| Dutch Angle | Unease | 3–8s | "Frame tilted 20 degrees counterclockwise. Maintain throughout." |
| Push-In Zoom + Dolly | Compression | 2–4s | "Simultaneous dolly forward 5 feet AND zoom to 85mm over 3 seconds." |
| Parallax Pan | Depth layers | 2–4s | "Pan left 20 degrees over 3 seconds. Foreground full pan, midground 65%, background 35%." |
| Push-In + Reveal | Hidden info | 2–4s | "Dolly forward 10 feet at 3 feet/second. Foreground object gradually reveals hidden background subject." |
| Whip Transition | Invisible seam | 0.4–0.7s | "Whip pan/blur from Scene A to Scene B. Subject A exits left with blur, Subject B enters right." |
| Lock-Off Static | Observational | 2–6s | "Camera locked. Zero movement. Subject moves through frame. f/2.8." |
| Reverse Parallax | Unsettling | 3–5s | "Pan right 30 degrees. Foreground 20% of pan, background 100%." |

Combine 2–3 movements in one clip: "Open with dolly forward (0–2s) + whip pan (2–2.3s) + tracking shot (2.3–5s)."

## Composition

- **Rule of thirds:** subject at grid intersections; horizon on top or bottom third
- **Leading lines:** roads, fences, ridges converge toward subject
- **Negative space:** subject occupies ~15–40% of frame
- **Frame within frame:** window, branches, doorway
- **Asymmetry** for tension; symmetry for formality
