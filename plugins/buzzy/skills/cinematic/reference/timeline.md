# Cinematic — Timeline Segmentation

按已冻结的 `VIDEO_DURATION` 选一档节拍。手填秒数就近靠这四档改写，不要发明 30s+ 结构。

## 4-Second（Maximum Compression）

**Hook + Transition + Payoff**

- **0–1.5s:** Extreme hook
- **1.5–3s:** Single main action
- **3–4s:** Climax hold, freeze, or cut to black

Beat: Impact → Build → Release

```
4-second compact cinematic moment. 0–0.8s: Black screen, silence.
At 0.8s: explosive light burst from center. 0.8–3s:
Single sustained action (character turn, object fall, landscape reveal).
3–4s: Climax hold with subtle motion, ends on strong visual punctuation.
no background music
```

## 8-Second（Standard）

**Hook + Exposition + Climax + Resolution**

- **0–2s:** Hook
- **2–4.5s:** Establishing or character introduction
- **4.5–7s:** Escalation
- **7–8s:** Climax / reveal

Pacing: slow first 3s → faster 4–6s → settle 6–8s

```
8-second cinematic arc. First 2s establishes hook: [hook technique].
2–4.5s: medium-paced camera movement revealing context. 4.5–7s:
escalating action with increasing movement speed.
7–8s: climax moment held for emphasis, slight motion blur for drama.
no background music
```

## 10-Second

**Hook + Setup + Rising Action + Climax + Denouement**

- **0–2s:** Hook
- **2–3.5s:** Location/character (slower)
- **3.5–6s:** First action
- **6–8.5s:** Escalation
- **8.5–10s:** Climax and resolution

```
10-second cinematic storytelling arc. 0–2s: hook captures attention
immediately. 2–3.5s: camera establishes location at calm pace (2 ft/s).
3.5–6s: introduce primary action, increase camera velocity to 3.5 ft/s.
6–8.5s: escalate tension, accelerate to 5 ft/s. 8.5–10s: climax, slight
deceleration, resolve emotional arc. no background music
```

## 15-Second（Full Narrative Arc）

**Hook + Exposition + Rising Action + Climax + Falling Action + Resolution**

- **0–2s:** Extreme hook
- **2–4.5s:** Establishing
- **4.5–7s:** Introduce subject
- **7–10s:** Rising action
- **10–12.5s:** Climax
- **12.5–15s:** Resolution

Velocity: 0–4.5s at 2–2.5 ft/s → 4.5–10s at 3–4 ft/s → 10–12.5s at 5+ ft/s or locked → 12.5–15s at 1–2 ft/s

```
15-second cinematic narrative. 0–2s: Hook moment. [specific hook].
2–4.5s: Establishing wide shot of [location]. Camera 50 feet back,
24mm equivalent. Slow pan at 2 ft/s. Warm three-point lighting.
4.5–7s: Introduce protagonist through medium shot. Dolly forward
2 feet at 2.5 ft/s. Depth of field f/2.8. 7–15s: continue rising
action, climax, resolution per the beat list above.
no background music
```
