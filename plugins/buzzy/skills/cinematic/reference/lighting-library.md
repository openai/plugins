# Cinematic — Lighting & Atmosphere

写词必须指定 key / fill / rim、色温、阴影软硬。不要写 "normal lighting"。

## Setups

| Lighting Setup | Mood / Genre | Intensity & Ratio | Prompt phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Three-Point Classic** | Controlled, universal | Key 100%, Fill 33%, Back 60% | "Three-point setup: warm 3000K key light at 45° left, intensity 100%. Soft fill at 60° right, 33% (3:1). Rim/back at 80° behind, 60%. Shadows have warm spill, no hard edges." |
| **Chiaroscuro / Noir** | Mystery, danger | Key 100%, Fill 10–15%, Back 40% | "High-contrast chiaroscuro: hard 3000K key from 45° left. Fill 10%. Rim 40%. 85% of frame in shadow. Film noir. Blacks crushed." |
| **Silhouette Backlit** | Power, isolation | Key 0%, Fill 0%, Back 100% | "Complete silhouette: subject backlit by bright 5000K background (window, fire, sunset). Zero fill. Pure black shape, rim defines outline." |
| **Golden Hour** | Romance, nostalgia | Key 100%, Fill 50%, Back 60%, 3000–3500K | "Golden hour: warm orange-amber 3000K at low 15° angle. Atmospheric haze. Shadows warm 2000K spill. Fill 50%. Entire frame golden." |
| **Cool Moonlit** | Isolation, night | Key 100% cool, Fill 20%, Back 30%, 6000–7500K | "Moonlight: directional cool 6500K at 30°. Shadows blue-tinted. Fill 20%. Low intensity." |
| **Practical Neon / Cyberpunk** | Dystopian, sci-fi | Split-color key, Fill 30% | "Practical neon visible in frame: hot-pink, cyan, acidic-green spill. Multiple conflicting shadows. Flicker optional." |
| **Soft Overcast** | Calm, vulnerable | Flat 100%, 5500K | "Soft overcast daylight: diffused uniform illumination, no harsh shadows. Soft-edge falloff. Peaceful, clear." |
| **Volumetric / God Rays** | Spiritual, grandeur | Directional key + particles | "Volumetric beams: 3000K key through particle-filled air. Dust motes in shafts. Lens flare at source." |
| **Fluorescent / Institutional** | Clinical, unease | Flat 100%, 4500–5000K | "Fluorescent institutional: cool slightly-greenish 4500K. Even overhead. Slight flicker 2–3 Hz. No shadows. Clinical, unsettling." |
| **Firelight / Candlelit** | Intimate, primal | Flicker, 1500–2000K | "Firelight: warm orange-red 1800K from visible fire. Flicker 2–4 Hz. Large dancing shadows. Warm spill in shadows." |
| **Harsh Midday Sun** | Heat, exposure | Key 100%, Fill 10% | "Harsh direct sun: 5500K at 60°. Contrast 10:1+. Hard-edged shadows. Heat shimmer." |
| **Two-Source Split** | Conflict, duality | Two keys, opposite color | "Split-source: warm 3000K key left 100%, cool 6000K back right 100%. Color-temperature conflict on the face." |
| **Low-Key Dramatic** | Suspense | Key 100%, Fill 20–30%, Back 80% | "Low-key: hard 3000K key at 45°. Fill 20–30%. Back 80%. Shadows occupy 60–70% of frame." |
| **High-Key Beauty** | Optimistic, safe | Key 100%, Fill 80–100%, Back 40% | "High-key beauty: warm soft key 45° 100%. Fill 80% (1.25:1). Rim 40%. Shadows nearly absent." |
| **Practical Tungsten Household** | Domestic realism | 2700K lamp + ambient fill | "Practical tungsten: visible warm 2700K table lamp as primary. Window ambient 20–30% fill. Lived-in, intimate." |

## Atmosphere add-ons

- **Haze:** "Thick atmospheric haze obscures 40% of background. Light diffuses through particles."
- **Grain:** "35mm film-stock grain, especially in shadows. Color fringing at high-contrast edges."
- **Bokeh:** "Shallow DOF, circular bokeh from practical lights. Foreground tack-sharp."
- **Motion blur:** "Background-blur 15% opacity, subject sharp. Trails on fast objects."
