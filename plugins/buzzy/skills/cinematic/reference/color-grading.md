# Cinematic — Color Grading Presets

写词指定色相 / 饱和 / 阴影高光分离。不要写 "cinematic color" 了事。

| Color Grade | Visual | Mood | Prompt phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Teal & Orange** | Cyan shadows, orange highlights | Modern, high-stakes | "Teal-orange modern cinema palette. Shadows cyan-teal (200° hue, 100% sat). Highlights orange-gold (30° hue, 90% sat). Midtones neutral. Overall saturation 110%." |
| **Desaturated + Accent** | 30% world, one color 100% | Symbolic focus | "Reduce overall saturation to 30% grey-blue. Keep 100% saturation on accent color only [red/gold/cyan]." |
| **Golden Hour / Warm Nostalgia** | 3200K amber | Romantic, wistful | "Color temperature 3200K amber-gold. Highlights warm orange-gold. Shadows warm orange-brown (not blue). Saturation +15% on warm tones." |
| **Cool Blue / Isolation** | 6500K, blue shadows | Lonely, sci-fi | "Color temperature 6500K. Shadows blue-cyan. Highlights slight warmth 4000K. Overall desaturation 85%." |
| **Bleach Bypass** | Lifted blacks, grain | Gritty, analog | "Bleach bypass: lift black point. Blacks dark-grey (value 10% not 0%). Greys elevated. Grain 150%. Vintage film stock." |
| **Desaturated Vintage** | 70% sat, lifted shadows | Classic, faded | "Vintage film: saturation 70%. Lift shadows. Warm shift +200K. 35mm grain. Slightly faded colors." |
| **High Contrast B&W** | 0% sat, crushed blacks | Stark, timeless | "Pure black-and-white: 0% saturation. Crush blacks to 0%. Lift whites. S-curve. Eliminate mid-tones." |
| **Cyberpunk Neon** | 140% sat, split-tone glow | Futuristic, intense | "Saturation 140%, vibrance 120%. Shadows cyan, highlights magenta-pink. Glow/bloom on light sources." |
| **Monochromatic Single-Color** | One hue only | Symbolic, meditative | "Convert to B&W, reintroduce one hue [red/blue/green/gold] at 100% only in matching tonal areas." |
| **Warm-Cool Split** | Warm FG + cool BG | Duality, day-for-night | "Foreground 2700K orange spill. Background 6500K blue-cyan. ~4000K temperature separation." |
