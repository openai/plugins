# Cinematic — Common Mistakes & Fixes

写完词用本表自检，改完再打印。

**1. 没有 2 秒钩子**
慢开场会丢人。「先建立漂亮风景」用 4 秒才入戏 = 失败。
Fix: 前两行写清钩子。"Open with black screen to light burst at 0.8s" 好过 "Open with establishing shot."

**2. 锁死机位全程不动**
Fix: 每 4 秒至少 1–2 个有意运动。"Camera dolly forward 3 feet at 2 ft/s" 好过 "camera moves forward."

**3. 平光**
Fix: 指定 45° key、fill 比（至少 3:1）、rim。"Three-point lighting with key 3000K 45° left" 好过 "normal lighting."

**4. 焦点乱漂**
Fix: "Focus locked on subject eyes throughout clip, depth of field f/1.4."

**5. 声画对不齐**
Fix: "At 5.5s, visual impact SYNCHRONIZED with the door slam." 不要把配乐峰值写进成片词。

**6. 画面太挤**
Fix: "Subject occupies 40% frame-left, 60% negative space right."

**7. 饱和推过头**
Fix: "Saturation 115%, teal shadows 200° hue, orange highlights 30° hue" 好过 "super saturated."

**8. 运镜过快**
默认 2–3 ft/s dolly、20–30°/s pan。5+ ft/s 只给高潮。
Fix: "2 ft/s constant" 或写明加速区间。

**9. 没有大气层**
Fix: "Volumetric light visible in dust particles, atmospheric haze 20% opacity."

**10. 对白盖过画面 / 写了 BGM**
Fix: 对白服务画面。成片词禁止 BGM。末尾 `no background music`。用户要配乐 → merge 闸门。

**11. 还在用 Higgsfield 语法**
`@material[name]` / 写死 720p / 「Seedance on Higgsfield」= 本步失败。改成 `<<<image_n>>>` + Session 分辨率。
