# 3D CGI — Render Styles

主风格只选 **1–2** 档。混用必须写清哪一层写实、哪一层 stylized。写入 `RenderStyle`。

## Primary families

- **Pixar / stylized**: warm saturated color, smooth shading, exaggerated proportions. "Pixar-style 3D render, Disney animation aesthetic, smooth cartoon shading, stylized proportions."
- **Photorealistic**: physical materials, SSS skin, fabric wrinkles, dust. "Photorealistic 3D render, physically accurate, cinema-camera aesthetic."
- **Low-poly**: faceted, flat or simple gradients. "Low-poly 3D, geometric faceted style, flat-shaded."
- **Claymation / plasticine**: handmade, finger marks, tactile. "Claymation aesthetic, plasticine texture, stop-motion 3D."
- **Isometric**: 30–45° locked perspective. "Isometric 3D view, 45-degree perspective."
- **Cel-shaded**: bold outlines, limited palette, hard shadow boundaries. "Cel-shaded rendering, graphic novel style."
- **Voxel**: cubic, retro. "Voxel-based 3D, cubic blocky style."
- **Wireframe / technical**: neon lines, no fills. "Neon wireframe, technical blueprint."
- **Hybrid**: "Photorealistic product in a stylized environment" — say which is which.

## Spectrum (hyper-stylized → photoreal)

1. Ultra-stylized cartoon — bright primaries, toon shading, 10:1 head ratio
2. Soft-shaded stylized — Pixar range, warm 3-point / HDRI
3. Painterly / illustrative — brush strokes, watercolor finish
4. Stylized realism — real proportions, surreal color or world
5. Cel-shaded with depth — outlines + rim + specular
6. Low-poly realistic — faceted geo, PBR materials
7. Isometric with detail — locked 45°, rich textures
8. Cinematic hyperreal — high-poly, GI, pore-level
9. Photoreal / digital photography — lens artifacts allowed as look, not a resolution promise
10. Hyper-photoreal + impossible physics — real light on surreal forms

不要写 "photorealistic but stylized" 而不拆层。
