# 3D CGI — 2-Second Hook Framework

写词第一句必须是视觉钩子。后续时间把钩子展开成揭示，不要先铺垫再入戏。

## Hook Techniques

1. **Dramatic Camera Fly-Through**
Impossible geometry at speed. "Camera hurtles through a crystalline tunnel, refracting light into fractals as we move."

2. **Scale Reveal (Tiny to Massive)**
Extreme closeup then pull back. "Begin on a single raindrop on a leaf. Pull back to a forest canopy, then a continent in a thunderstorm."

3. **Impossible Camera Angle**
Inside glass, on surface tension, through a cross-section. "Camera rotates around a spinning hourglass, passing through its surface to show the sand in realtime."

4. **Object Materializing from Particles**
Dust / light / data coalesce. "Golden particles swirl and settle into a detailed humanoid form."

5. **Photoreal Object in Surreal Setting**
"A pristine crystal crown sits on a floor of clouds while aurora swirls overhead."

6. **Transformative Morph**
"A marble rolls across white space, morphing mid-motion into a silver sphere, then a water droplet."

7. **Revealing Through Transparency**
"A stone statue cracks; light pours through fissures; the exterior dissolves to a molten core."

8. **Depth Shift / DOF**
"Focus on a dust particle in light. Rack to vast interior architecture behind it."

9. **Chromatic Separation & Refraction**
"A prism catches sunlight, fragmenting the frame into spectrum around the product."

10. **Kinetic Decomposition**
"A watch face dissolves into floating gears and springs, each rotating slowly."

11. **Environmental Reaction**
"A hand emerges from water. Concentric ripples. Caustics dance across the palm."

12. **Temporal Speed Manipulation**
"A flower blooms in extreme slow-motion for 3s, then a garden blooms in 2s fast-motion."

从问询 `HookPlan` 取主钩子；没选就按 `RenderStyle` + brief 挑一个，不要每片都用粒子凝聚。
