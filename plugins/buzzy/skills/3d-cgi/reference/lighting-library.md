# 3D CGI — Lighting Encyclopedia

必须写 key / fill / rim（或明确没有）、色温、阴影软硬。不要写 "nice lighting"。

## Terms

- **HDRI**: "Outdoor HDRI, warm golden-hour" / "studio HDRI, soft diffuse."
- **3-point**: warm key, cool fill, crisp rim.
- **GI**: color bleeding, bounce.
- **Caustics**: water / glass patterns.
- **God rays**: volumetric through dust/fog.
- **Practical**: neon, candle, screens in-frame.

## Setups

1. **Golden Hour HDRI** — 3200K key 45° UL, cool blue fill, sunset HDRI. Outdoor product / nature / beauty.
2. **Studio Neutral 3-Point** — 5600K soft key 45°, fill to ~70% shadow, crisp rear rim. Product / corporate.
3. **Single Dramatic Side-Light** — 80° key, almost no fill. Noir / horror.
4. **Rim-Dominant** — minimal key, luminous outline. Magical reveal.
5. **Cool Moonlight** — 8000K+ UL, deep shadows, dark HDRI. Night / sci-fi.
6. **Warm & Cool Contrast** — 3000K left vs 8000K right. Fashion / ads.
7. **All-Around Soft HDRI** — no hard shadows. Detail / documentation.
8. **Volumetric God Rays** — strong directional + heavy fog/dust. Architecture / magic.
9. **Underwater Caustics** — top sun, dancing floor patterns, water HDRI.
10. **Neon Practical** — magenta/cyan/yellow in-scene, dark city HDRI. Cyberpunk.
11. **Natural Window** — north/overcast window, interior bounce. Interiors.
12. **Harsh Midday Sun** — high sun, crisp shadows, little fill. Clarity shots.
13. **Candlelight** — 2000K low practical, warm bounce. Intimate.
14. **Cool Overcast + Warm Fill** — 6000K sky + gold bounce. Contemporary.
15. **Extreme Backlight** — zero key, full halo. Silhouette intro.

写清：光源是自然 / 人工 / 场景内 practical；角度；Kelvin；fill 比；阴影 penumbra；有无焦散 / 体积光。
