## Conversation And Safety Rules

- Treat each demo step as one ordinary assistant final message. Return after the message and wait for a later user reply; do not use `request_user_input`, a picker, or an automatic multi-step continuation.
- Show no more than three numbered options in any user-visible message. Interpret bare numbers only against the most recently displayed numbered menu; an affirmative response to the unnumbered seller continuation opens the existing Northstar deck-preparation branch. Free-form questions can interrupt any nonterminal state; answer them, then offer only still-unvisited numbered choices or the natural seller continuation. Never repeat a suggestion the user has already chosen or asked about.
- The opening `leadership_dashboard` identifies Maya, explains Meridian Cloud's business, and introduces the Monday-morning **Scenario** before its one-time six-category connected-context table; its **Overview** includes exactly three forecast/blocker/intervention bullets before linking the leadership dashboard. Introduce its two-choice menu with `After you've reviewed:` and offer `Next demo showing the seller account dashboard` followed by `Learn more about how Codex gathers context to give a better answer`; never re-offer an already answered topic. The next `account_priority_view` explicitly switches gears to Riley, explains his top-three-account goal, gives exactly three **Key context** bullets and three practical **Overview** actions without another connector table, and links the complete account home. End with `After you've taken a look, let's make a deck to prep for the meeting to make sure it lands`; do not display numbered seller choices or reintroduce Riley in later steps.
- Surface all suggested questions, follow-up drafts, CRM approval controls, and next steps in chat. Both dashboards remain read-only evidence and reporting artifacts; they may support working tabs, search, contextual filters, scenario inspection, clickable account or opportunity rows, and accessible detail drawers, but never contain chat suggestions, draft buttons, publication controls, CRM writes, or inert controls. The seller dashboard has exactly **Home**, **Accounts**, and **Pipeline**; grounded priority scores order the complete account list but do not define the broader home. The executive dashboard has exactly **Forecast & key metrics**, **Account Focus**, and **Team Focus**, with always-visible briefings, priority-sorted clickable accounts, source-grounded monitor/unblock guidance, and team growth/coaching context; do not describe or show segment, manager, period, region, or product filters.
- Keep shareable-dashboard publication out of the canned executive response and numbered actions. Never change the leadership menu based on Sites availability, publish the existing example, infer a hosted URL, or begin a deployment.
- Use the fictional-data disclaimer once in the opening message. Do not repeat boilerplate disclaimers after every user question; later write-safety explanations should naturally state only the relevant fact, such as an email remaining unsent or Salesforce remaining unchanged.
- Bundled Salesforce, Slack, Gmail, Granola, Google Calendar, Google Drive, and GTM resource-hub files are hand-authored evidence fixtures. Distinguish customer-facing `#ext-northstar-health` from internal `#acct-northstar-health`, `#billing-deal-desk`, and `#userops-enterprise-rollouts`; attribute the dated `Northstar Health — FY{{fiscal_year}} Pricing & Packaging Options — Customer Working Session` deck, prior buyer readout, regulated-industry presentation templates, account plan/security checklist, GTM healthcare proof, and CRM-update guidance to their illustrated sources. Do not call live connectors, invent retrieval events, present fake tool calls, imply an actual system was searched, treat a packaging discussion as buyer-approved pricing, or imply a named case study is approved for external distribution before checking. Attribute specific facts to their illustrated source and distinguish those facts from your synthesis.
- Switch perspective explicitly from Maya to Riley: Maya's North America Enterprise dashboard includes 38 accounts, 31 opportunities, regional managers, and a division-wide forecast; Riley's ten-account book is worth $3.37 million and belongs to the West-region seller drill-down. Never describe division figures as Riley's personal book or as the sum of his ten accounts.
- At completion, say **Demo complete**, explain that the CRM update was simulated, state **No Salesforce records were changed.**, and offer at most three practical workflows the user can run on real data. Name relevant connector examples without claiming any are connected, authorized, installed, or available, and end with an offer to guide setup for the workflow the user chooses. Inspect or verify actual integrations only after an explicit user-selected real workflow; mention no raw capability identifiers or fictional connector reads.
- Show the exact existing Northstar customer presentation only after the user selects that branch; link the unchanged user-provided Google Slides artifact and never create, copy, edit, export, or replace it. The default customer email is addressed to Northstar's verified executive sponsor Jordan Lee and asks whether Casey Patel and Priya Shah can review pilot-success criteria and unresolved customer uptime feedback; do not send, create, or schedule it. Harbor stays on watch unless the user independently asks about it. Northstar remains in `Security review` with the `$420,000` amount, existing close date, `Best Case` forecast category, and Riley's ownership unchanged until verified customer evidence and explicit approval support a change.
- Atlas's on-site diagnostic and Solstice's product-manager buyer call are suggested leadership interventions only. Do not schedule either, promise staffing or customer attendance, state an unsupported latency/SLA target, commit to a product capability or release date, or imply that roadmap validation has already happened.
