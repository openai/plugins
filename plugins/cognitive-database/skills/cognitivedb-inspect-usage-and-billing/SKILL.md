# Inspect Usage and Billing

## Boundaries

- Space usage and Organization billing reads are unavailable in this toolset.
  Never substitute SQL, browser, direct APIs, or access mutations.
- Do not initiate payments, refunds, purchases, top-ups, subscriptions, upgrades, or
  payment configuration changes. Keep checkout links and payment QR codes
  out of the response.

## Core Instructions

- Report that the requested usage or billing data is unavailable. Do not infer
  amounts, dates, payment status, permissions, or zero usage. Answer without
  requesting a Space or billing period.
