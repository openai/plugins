# Exact Content

Use when copy, chart data, labels, numbers, claims, logos, dimensions, legal text, or approved brand content must remain exact.

Do not let image generation invent, rewrite, relabel, reorder, hide, or hallucinate exact content.

Use deterministic composition, overlays, or exports for exact text, data, logos, dimensions, and safe zones. Image generation may provide visual treatment, backgrounds, materials, lighting, and composition only.
