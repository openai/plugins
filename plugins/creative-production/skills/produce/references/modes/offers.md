# Offers

Use when the user wants to compare how a product, service, venue, package, event, or offer should be visually presented.

Explore useful families such as product hero, product in use, ecommerce presentation, landing-page hero, retail placement, event treatment, feature or proof treatment, and editorial treatment.

For pack-driven route generation, use the reusable packs and builder under `../../helpers/offers/`.

Do not invent pricing, claims, certifications, customer quotes, or product facts.
